How it works

From one recording to a working flow

No workshops and no process interviews. Do the work once, the way you always do it.

  1. 1

    Record the task, or upload a video

    Click Record and work as usual. Tick Narrate to say why as you go: "the due date is the invoice date plus 30 days". Already have a screen video? Upload it instead, up to 60 minutes.

  2. 2

    FlowSME maps what happened

    It reads every screen change: the app, the field or cell, and whether you typed, copied or pasted. It splits the work into tasks, finds the routines you repeat and follows each value from one app to the next.

  3. 3

    Get the report, the SOP and the flow

    Each task gets an automation potential score, minutes saved, hours and money saved a month, and a payback time. Pick a task and FlowSME writes the flow for your workflow tool.

Privacy

It sees how the work flows, not what you typed

FlowSME needs the shape of the work: which app, which field, where a value went. Not the words.

What FlowSME uses

  • The app, the window and what you clicked: the button, field or cell.
  • How much you typed: the number of characters and the shortcuts, not the text.
  • Value fingerprints: a salted one-way fingerprint of each value you copy or type.
  • The active window only: that window, plus any menu or dialog in front of it, is what goes to the AI.

What it never captures or sends

  • The text you type. Password fields are never captured at all.
  • Password windows: with a password manager, a Windows security prompt or a sign-in window in front, nothing is captured. No screenshot, no title, no text.
  • Your other windows, such as mail and chats, are never sent to the AI.
  • The microphone, unless you choose to narrate. Ctrl+Alt+P pauses everything.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Something else? Write to hello@flowsme.example.

What does FlowSME need from me?

A Windows PC and one process you do often. Record it with FlowSME Recorder, or send a screen video you already have from Teams, Loom, Snipping Tool, OBS or Xbox Game Bar, up to 60 minutes and 1 GB.

Is a screen video as good as a recording?

Close, but less exact, and the report says so. Step times are approximate, and copying between apps is inferred from what was visible instead of proven by value fingerprints. In our own test, FlowSME found 47 of the 50 screen changes in a 5.6-minute video.

How accurate are the hours-saved figures?

They are estimates from what was recorded, priced at the hourly rate for the person's position and how often the process runs. One recording shows one path through a process, so record it a few times to see the exceptions.

Which automation tools does FlowSME build for?

n8n, Make, Power Automate, Node-RED, Azure Logic Apps and Windmill, plus Postman, SQL and Playwright starters. FlowSME can create the flow in your tool through its API, switched off until you review it and turn it on.

Does FlowSME see my passwords or what I type?

No. Typed text is not stored, only how many characters you typed and which shortcuts you used. Password fields, password managers, Windows security prompts and sign-in windows are never captured.

What does the AI cost?

Very little. In our own test, reading a 5.6-minute narrated video and writing the report used about $0.30 of AI. A routine that repeats is described once and reused, which keeps long recordings cheap.

Does it work on a Mac?

FlowSME Recorder runs on Windows. For work done on another computer, record it with any screen recorder and send the video instead.

Can I record my team's work?

Yes, with their consent. Each employee gets a seat, records their own work and sends it to you. Check the local rules on employee monitoring, such as GDPR or works councils.
