How it works

Three steps from a recording to a working flow

  1. 1

    Record the task, or upload a video

    Click Record and do the work as usual. Tick Narrate to say why as you go: "the due date is the invoice date plus 30 days". Already have a screen video? Upload it instead, up to 60 minutes.

  2. 2

    FlowSME maps what happened

    It reads every screen change: the app, the field or cell, and whether you typed, copied or pasted. It splits the work into tasks, finds the routines you repeat and follows each value from one app to the next.

  3. 3

    Get the report, the SOP and the flow

    Each task gets an automation potential score, minutes saved per run, hours and money saved a month, and a payback time. Pick a task and FlowSME writes the flow for your workflow tool.

Illustrative example.

Managers and employees

Your team records. You see the savings.

A manager sets up the team once. Employees record their own work and send it in. Nobody types an hourly cost into a report.

Manager
  • Create the team with its number of seats, then add each person with their position.
  • Each person gets a seat file to open once. Revoke a seat and it stops working; restore it when one is free.
  • Set an hourly rate for each position. Every report uses it to price the time saved.
  • See the recordings employees sent in one inbox, and compare several people doing the same process.
Employee
  • Open the seat file once. The app shows your name, position and team.
  • Record your work, pause for anything private, and send it to your manager.
  • Hourly rates are never shown to you. They stay on your manager's computer, and the shared team folder holds no names or rates.

Rates stay with the manager. Employees see their own work and results, never what their time is costed at.

Privacy

It sees how the work flows, not what you typed

Typed text is not stored

FlowSME keeps how many characters you typed and the shortcuts you used, not the words. Password fields are never captured.

Values become fingerprints

When you copy or type a value, FlowSME keeps a salted one-way fingerprint of it, never the value itself. Matching fingerprints prove the vendor typed into the portal is the one copied from the PDF. The salt is never saved, so fingerprints can't be reversed.

Only the active window goes to the AI

Each screenshot sent to the AI is cut to the window you are working in, plus any menu or dialog in front of it. Your mail and chats in other windows never reach it.

Password windows are excluded

When a password manager, a Windows security prompt or a sign-in window is in front, nothing is captured: no screenshot, no title, no text.

The microphone is off unless you choose to narrate, and Ctrl+Alt+P pauses everything. If you record other people's work, get their consent and check the local rules on employee monitoring, such as GDPR or works councils.

Muse Spark

Reads the screen and writes the analysis

Muse Spark looks at each screen change to see what you did, then writes the analysis, the SOP and the recommendations. A routine that repeats is described once and reused, which keeps long recordings cheap.

Muse Voice Transcribe

Muse Voice Transcribe turns what you say while you work into text. Any rule FlowSME takes from your narration must quote your exact words, or it is rejected.

FAQ

Questions people ask

What does FlowSME need from me?

A Windows PC and one process you do often. Record it with FlowSME Recorder, or send a screen video you already have from Teams, Loom, Snipping Tool, OBS or Xbox Game Bar, up to 60 minutes and 1 GB.

Is a screen video as good as a recording?

Close, but less exact, and the report says so. Step times are approximate, and copying between apps is inferred from what was visible instead of proven by value fingerprints. In our own test, FlowSME found 47 of the 50 screen changes in a 5.6-minute video.

How accurate are the hours-saved figures?

They are estimates from what was recorded, priced at the hourly rate for the person's position and how often the process runs. One recording shows one path through a process, so record it a few times to see the exceptions.

Which automation tools does FlowSME build for?

n8n, Make, Power Automate, Node-RED, Azure Logic Apps and Windmill, plus Postman, SQL and Playwright starters. FlowSME can create the flow in your tool through its API, switched off until you review it and turn it on.

Does FlowSME see my passwords or what I type?

No. Typed text is not stored, only how many characters you typed and which shortcuts you used. Password fields, password managers, Windows security prompts and sign-in windows are never captured.

What does the AI cost?

Very little. In our own test, reading a 5.6-minute narrated video and writing the report used about $0.30 of AI. A routine that repeats is described once and reused, which keeps long recordings cheap.

Does it work on a Mac?

FlowSME Recorder runs on Windows. For work done on another computer, record it with any screen recorder and send the video instead.

Can I record my team's work?

Yes, with their consent. Each employee gets a seat, records their own work and sends it to you. Check the local rules on employee monitoring, such as GDPR or works councils.
